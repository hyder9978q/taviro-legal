Taviro

Taviro is a private system that produces short Arabic videos about phone settings, using AI at work and Iraqi e-government services, each claim taken from a saved official source. It publishes them only to its owner's own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and Telegram accounts, and only after the owner approves each post. It has no users other than the owner and collects no data about viewers.
